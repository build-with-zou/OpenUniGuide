# Center for Intelligent Sensor and MEMS

## 基础信息

- **所属学校**： National University of Singapore
- **所属院系**： Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, College of Design and Engineering
- **实验室/中心名称**： Center for Intelligent Sensor and MEMS (CISM)
- **主页链接**： https://cde.nus.edu.sg/ece/research/research-centers/cism/

## 研究方向

- **方向 1**： Intelligent sensors and MEMS/NEMS
- **方向 2**： Micro/nanotechnology and wafer-level packaging
- **方向 3**： Photonics ICs, sensors and optical MEMS/NEMS
- **方向 4**： Low-power ICs and energy harvesters
- **方向 5**： Healthcare technology, biomedical sensors and microfluidics
- **方向 6**： Flexible/stretchable electronics and 2D materials
