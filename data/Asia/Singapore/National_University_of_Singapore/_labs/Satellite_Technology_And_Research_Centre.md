# Satellite Technology And Research Centre

## 基础信息

- **所属学校**： National University of Singapore
- **所属院系**： Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, College of Design and Engineering
- **实验室/中心名称**： Satellite Technology And Research Centre (STAR)
- **主页链接**： https://cde.nus.edu.sg/ece/research/research-centers/star/

## 研究方向

- **方向 1**： Miniaturized satellites and CubeSat platforms
- **方向 2**： Formation, constellation and swarm flying
- **方向 3**： Inter-satellite communication payloads
- **方向 4**： Satellite attitude, propulsion, navigation and control
- **方向 5**： Maritime VDES payloads and space-based communication
- **方向 6**： Space environmental testing and mission control facilities
