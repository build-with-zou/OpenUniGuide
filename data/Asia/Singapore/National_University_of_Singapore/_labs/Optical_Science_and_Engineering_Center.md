# Optical Science and Engineering Center

## 基础信息

- **所属学校**： National University of Singapore
- **所属院系**： Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, College of Design and Engineering
- **实验室/中心名称**： Optical Science and Engineering Center (OSEC)
- **主页链接**： https://cde.nus.edu.sg/ece/research/research-centers/osec/

## 研究方向

- **方向 1**： Optical science and engineering
- **方向 2**： Laser nanofabrication
- **方向 3**： Laser precision engineering for industrial applications
- **方向 4**： Nanophotonics
- **方向 5**： Optical diagnostics
- **方向 6**： Technology commercialization and student training in optical fields
