# Robotics and Embodied Artificial Intelligence Lab

## 基础信息

- **所属学校**： Stanford University
- **所属院系**： Department of Electrical Engineering, School of Engineering；by courtesy, Computer Science
- **实验室/研究组名称**： Robotics and Embodied Artificial Intelligence Lab (REAL@Stanford / REALab)
- **主页链接**： https://real.stanford.edu/

## 研究方向

- **方向 1**： Robotics and embodied AI
- **方向 2**： Robot learning from interaction with the physical world
- **方向 3**： Perception and manipulation skills
- **方向 4**： Autonomous execution of complex tasks
- **方向 5**： Visuomotor policies and sensorimotor learning
- **方向 6**： Learning from human demonstrations, physics simulators, real-world interactions, and internet data
