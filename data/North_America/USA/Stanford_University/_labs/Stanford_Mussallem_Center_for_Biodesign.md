# Stanford Mussallem Center for Biodesign

## 基础信息

- **所属学校**：Stanford University
- **所属单位**：Stanford Medicine；与 Stanford Bio-X、Stanford Engineering、Stanford Business 和 Silicon Valley health technology ecosystem 交叉
- **实验室/中心名称**：Stanford Mussallem Center for Biodesign
- **主页链接**：https://biodesign.stanford.edu/

## 研究方向

- **方向 1**：Need-driven health technology innovation and biodesign innovation process
- **方向 2**：Medical device innovation、digital health、technology assessment、needs finding in healthcare
- **方向 3**：Health technology entrepreneurship、policy、global biodesign、health equity
- **方向 4**：Pediatric and maternal health technology, life sciences innovation, translational health innovation
- **方向 5**：Stanford courses、fellowships、grants、mentoring and networking for health technology projects

## 负责人和研究共同体

- **研究共同体**：Stanford Biodesign 为 Stanford students、fellows、faculty 和 experienced innovators 提供 health technology innovation training and support；不是单一 faculty lab
- **备注**：Bioengineering Research Centers & Facilities 页面也列出 Stanford Byers Center for Biodesign；中心官网当前名称为 Stanford Mussallem Center for Biodesign。具体课程、fellowship 和导师网络以 Stanford Biodesign 官网为准

## 相关项目

- **项目 1**：PhD in Bioengineering
