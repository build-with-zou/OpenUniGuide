# Stanford Center on Longevity

## 基础信息

- **所属学校**：Stanford University
- **所属单位**：University-wide interdisciplinary center
- **实验室/研究中心名称**：Stanford Center on Longevity
- **主页链接**：https://longevity.stanford.edu/

## 研究方向

- **方向 1**：The New Map of Life
- **方向 2**：Healthy, purposeful, engaged long lives
- **方向 3**：Decision science、self-regulation、learning and thriving across the lifespan
- **方向 4**：Cross-disciplinary research, education and public engagement on aging
- **方向 5**：Collaboration across generations and equity across the lifespan
