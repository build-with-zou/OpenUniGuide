# Computer Architecture Labs at Carnegie Mellon

## 基础信息

- **所属学校**： Carnegie Mellon University
- **所属院系**： Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, College of Engineering
- **实验室/研究组名称**： Computer Architecture Labs at Carnegie Mellon (CALCM)
- **主页链接**： https://research.ece.cmu.edu/~calcm/doku.php

## 研究方向

- **方向 1**： Computer architecture
- **方向 2**： Hardware/software co-design
- **方向 3**： Reconfigurable computing and programmable logic
- **方向 4**： Machine learning hardware and data center computing
- **方向 5**： Systems performance, runtime systems and compilation
